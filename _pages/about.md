## Biography
My name is Yingtao Luo (Ying-Tao Luo, 罗颖韬). I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Machine Learning (joint program) at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.ml.cmu.edu/people/phd-students.html). My research focuses on building adaptive and personalized AI systems that learn from interactions, reason over long-term context, and support complex decision-making.

## Research Interest
LLM Post-Training · Generative Retrieval · Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) · Personal Agents · Long-Horizon Sequence Learning · Reinforcement Learning

## Industry Experience
My work spans LLM agents, model training and post-training, and production-grade personalized generative retrieval systems. I have approximately two years of industry research experience across Roblox, Microsoft, Alibaba, and RealAI.

## Open to Opportunities
I'm always happy to connect about research collaborations, industry opportunities, startups, or interesting ideas!
